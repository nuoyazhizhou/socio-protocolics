# v11.2 Numbering, Migration, and Section Concordance

2026-10-26 | Current editorial candidate version. The 19 effective SP-RFC items are numbered consecutively 001—019; this does not mean the 19 items have been demonstrated, are minimal, or are complete. The six core proposition groups remain an index of questions; 007 is the cross-group normative rule and 019 is the Chapter 8 tooling/research norm.

## Changes in This Version

- The 006 of v11.1 is retained as the chapter-end candidate process diagram of Group II, and 010 as the chapter-end application note of Group III; both items leave the independent numbering and are not merged into other clauses.
- The 003 and 016 of v11.1 retain independent entry points; 021 is moved into Chapter 8 and continues with its own independent number. This version does not, on the basis of retained numbers, regard them as irreplaceable, as already empirically substantiated, or as carrying external normative force.
- The four "corollaries / laws" of the former 019 are changed into risk hypotheses, self-restraints and migration recommendations; the former 018 drops the overreaching final sentence that goes from message diffusion to organisational impossibility; the CheatSheet restores the general budget summation; the symbol scope points back to the actual game section.
- The postulates, the scope of the six groups, the reference-engine calculation methods and hypothetical simulation inputs have not acquired new verification results because of the changes in this version.

## Citation Rules

The current `sp-rfc-001` through `sp-rfc-019` use the v11.2 meaning. Old v11.1 numbers are tracked through `legacy-v11-1-rfc-XXX`, old v11.0 numbers through `legacy-v11-0-rfc-XXX`; an unversioned old link with the same number cannot guarantee the original meaning. Historical section positions use `legacy-v11-1-sec-X-Y`; current section numbers are governed by the main body text.
