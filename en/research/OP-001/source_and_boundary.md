## Identity of the Material

- S1—S3 and all numerical values are author-constructed; the conditional answers are within-scenario arithmetic, not social-measurement labels.
- A/B are self-written candidate checklists; A was not demoted to merely counting layers, nor was B treated as a verified tool.
- δ=0.10, the 95% interval, the character-length difference within 10%, and the 8–12-person pre-test example are all design choices; not constants derived from the postulates or a formal power analysis.
- OP-001 measures the local gain of method expression. The real net contribution of the intermediary, complexity feedback, and postulate applicability require other research.
- The original PDFs, download-failure records, old-draft differences and verification logs remain with the maintainer locally and are not distributed with the public pack. The public table records sources and limitations, and does not reproduce entire external literature or private audit material.
