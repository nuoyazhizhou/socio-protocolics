# Case Selection and Comparison Method: How to Let Both Success and Failure Correct the Framework

Version: exploration plan v0.4 | Date: 2026-10-23 | Language: Chinese original; English translation pending.

Status: an exploration plan formulated after some historical outcomes were already known, not a prediction made before the historical events occurred, not a formal pre-registration, and no cross-case coding or causal-effect estimation has been completed. This document stipulates how evidence is to be collated next; it cannot be used to claim that the four cases have verified the whitepaper.

## 0. Common rules and the Group I theme entry

[Case-Driven Writing and Proposition Testing](../CASE_DRIVEN_TESTING.md) applies to all six groups. The target sub-claims, real main cases, reverse material, judgement-change conditions, ordinary explanations, and theoretical write-backs of Group I are uniformly maintained in the testing card of the [Channel Capacity and Semantic Boundary theme plan](../research/topic_plan/I_channel_capacity_and_semantic_boundary.md); this page does not parallel-copy them, to avoid two versions appearing when continued.

What follows retains the selection method, existing candidates, and source-reading scope of the language-reform cases. Material registration does not equal effect verification; articles first unfold the real process, each submits a proposition receipt, and a conceptual sample chapter does not count as passing T3.

## I. Object of study and selection principles

The first-round scope is "how a written-expression or script-norm change aimed at a broad audience moves from advocacy into use." The goals of observers, reformers, and users may differ; first record the original actors' goals, then separately list this project's evaluation goals.

Both Chinese and foreign candidates of institutional adoption and blocked/withdrawn are included, so readers can compare different paths; this is a deliberately selected exploratory sample of differences, not random sampling, and is not used to estimate reform success rates. A case must not be deleted merely for not fitting the framework, nor may political stance, personal evaluation, or outcome goodness/badness be used as mechanism coding.

First register all actually-considered candidates, objects, periods, source conditions, inclusion/deferral reasons, and known results. Prioritise cases whose material is traceable, whose results can be layered, and for which opposing evidence can be found. If no qualified failure material exists, honestly retain the gap; do not fabricate a failure.

Hu Shi and the Second-Simplification are not the same kind of reform; the US 2010 Plain Writing Act and the 1906 spelling initiative are also not equivalent. The four cells are an organisational device for dissemination and exploration, not an experimental design that has already controlled for background. Only by adding same-field neighbours and intra-case differences can mechanisms be further distinguished.

## II. Define success and failure first; do not give the whole case one total label

Each case fixes its start and end points, object, region, document/textbook type, audience, implementer, and denominator; record dimension by dimension, forbidding substituting one item for another:

| Result dimension | What can be observed | What cannot be substituted |
|---|---|---|
| Formal entry | Publication, authorisation, scope, effectiveness, and withdrawal documents | Cannot substitute for real adoption or effect |
| Actual adoption | Number and population of adoptions in comparable textbooks, documents, periodicals, or institutions | Publication does not equal use; a sample does not equal the nation |
| Comprehension and task effect | Errors, accuracy, completion time, training requirement | Shorter text or fewer strokes does not equal easier comprehension |
| Persistence and compatibility | At the same time point, retention, mixing, rework, rollback, and replacement cost | Withdrawal does not equal that every word form or every goal failed |
| Distribution and accessibility | Who among old/new readers, teachers, publishers, institutions benefits and who bears the burden | Institutional savings do not equal overall cost decline |

The same case can be institutionally adopted successfully, with comprehension benefit undetermined, and migration cost on the high side. Windows preferentially list both the post-reform 1-year and 5-year available records; a window not yet reached or lacking records is marked missing, and zero-filling is forbidden. Also retain the key-event timeline. Different windows cannot be directly treated as a binary success/failure contrast; if material conditions force a window change, record the change and its reason.

## IV. How to study failure causes

First propose the mechanism and its observable consequences, then check the material. The relations in the table below are to-be-tested explanations, not already-ascertained causes.

| Candidate mechanism | Process evidence to seek | Observation that would weaken the explanation |
|---|---|---|
| Learning and comprehension cost | Training, misreading, rework, and correction records under comparable tasks; distinguish beginners from proficient users | Comprehension effects are similar while change mainly follows authorisation or budget shifts; the alleged cost reduction has only stroke/character-count basis |
| Compatibility and conversion cost | Conversion work among textbooks, toolbooks, font libraries, printing, and old/new users, stating who pays and when | Low-cost compatibility already exists and actual conversion burden is small, yet the same blockage still occurs; a contemporaneous non-conversion factor explains more strongly |
| Supporting measures and incentives | Training, material supply, adoption opportunity, and rejection behaviour; actual behaviour before and after formal recognition | Adoption absent despite support; continued adoption without formal recognition; interest change explains all the difference |
| Authorisation and coordination | Who can decide, who can block, scope of execution, and conflicting documents; distinguish actual power from the engineering-authority analogy | No presupposed conflict, or same coordination structure with different outcomes; change was triggered first by another shock |
| Feedback and adjustment | Timing and content of trials, complaints, revisions, suspensions, rollbacks | The same adjustment occurred without relying on the alleged feedback having happened; a "PDCA" label is pasted onto process only because of the outcome |
| External conditions | Independent timelines of political alignment, educational expansion, economic and media change | The alleged protocol factor only restates these changes, with no independent evidence or distinguishable meaning |

For CN-S2, the attached request report proposed reasons such as normative stability and existing toolbooks and font libraries; for now these can only be registered as reasons the decision-makers listed. To argue that they actually caused the suspension/abolition, one must supplement records of adoption and conversion difficulty that had already appeared, check revision, political, and organisational explanations, and seek departmental or user material opposing the claim.

One must not write all failures uniformly as "technical debt," "resistance," or "no racetrack." An authorisation dispute also cannot be renamed "compatibility cost" without cost and behavioural material. An explanation that can absorb all opposing results yet cannot point to what would make it fail is not yet a qualified case hypothesis.

## V. Practical steps for comparison and causal identification

1. **Build the timeline first.** Each record distinguishes event, actor explanation, and later research; sequence is necessary but not sufficient. A withdrawal document alone cannot retro-infer that all users opposed beforehand.
2. **Code by unified questions.** The two Chinese cases and two foreign cases use the same fields; the same material cited multiple times counts as one source chain. Links relying on only one side's explanation are separately marked as gaps.
3. **Do within-case comparison.** When conditions are comparable and material exists, look at differences across grades, departments, document types, glyphs, or pilots. The reason for selective entry must also be recorded; a pilot and a non-pilot cannot be naturally treated as random groups.
4. **Do neighbour comparison.** The Second-Simplification preferentially supplements the 1956 scheme as same-field material, avoiding only cross-era and cross-country contrast. Neighbour comparison still cannot automatically eliminate differences in education level, politics, and media conditions.
5. **Distinguish predictions.** Let the protocol explanation, conversion-cost/path-dependence, language policy, and organisational-incentive explanations each answer the same question, writing out under which observation a different judgement would be made. If the meaning is the same, register it as a restatement; do not force a new mechanism.
6. **Estimate only with qualified data.** If sufficient before/after records and credible comparisons are obtained, write a separate identification plan; the four historical stories cannot directly yield reliable regression, causal effect, or success-rate inference. When conditions are absent, report only process evidence, competing explanations, and uncertainty.

This round already knows the case outcomes; any analysis freeze only binds subsequent work and cannot be packaged as a prior blind prediction. Hereafter, when new material or new cases are added, record which outcomes/material were previously known; author analysis done leaving a blank is not to be passed off as independent blind testing.

## VI. How to judge whether the whitepaper adds value

Each case finally submits three columns: the specific questions the framework prompted checking, whether the ordinary explanation already covers them, and the new evidence or error correction brought. Assess separately:

- Expressive value: whether readers understand more easily; fair contrast is required, and one cannot score by the author's feeling or by likes.
- Methodological value: whether, under the same material, fewer denominators, conversion burdens, authorities, or counterexamples are missed; engineering representation may also increase misjudgement. The intermediary sample OP-001 belongs to this kind of candidate and is not to be confused with [PR-001](PR-001_agent_collaboration_scale.md).
- Mechanistic value: whether, relative to existing explanations, it yields an observation that is both distinguishable and supported; independent operationalisation and corresponding identification are required, and it is not automatically obtained by accumulating terms or case counts.

If the engineering formulation adds no judgement or prediction, narrow it to a pedagogical restatement; if it conflicts with evidence, revise or abandon the specific application. Retain records of the unexplainable, zero gain, and negative gain. One cannot rewrite both positive and negative results as support for the same proposition.

## VII. The take-up person's first deliverable

The [CN-S2 document and evidence table v0.2](cases/CN-S2_evidence_starting_point.md) already exists, explicitly registered as a reverse-test candidate for SP-RFC-001's over-strong stroke-saving extrapolation, rather than a measured cognitive counterexample; the goal is to separate fact from cause, without first writing a whole article. That table has only one official forwarding source chain and still lacks independent material. Continue supplementing evidence using the following fields:

`case_id, scope and window, source_id, original author/agency, drafting date, event date, document type, original sentence and locating, transcription/image status, result dimension, mechanism hypothesis, support or oppose, competing explanation, source chain, unknown items, coder and disagreement, version`

One may first extract from the located forwarding text the four kinds of records—proposal, trial-stop, revision, abolition—then supplement each item's cited original notice and contemporaneous feedback; if not found, also record the search scope. Missing numbers are marked NA, and administrative rationale and actual cost each get their own field. Accurate material and non-overstepping claims already constitute a useful deliverable; passing the whitepaper is not the acceptance condition.

Subsequently each case forms a Chinese article, source and boundary note, comparison table, and one concrete open task, then is translated per the [bilingual conventions](../BILINGUAL_CONVENTIONS.md). When a proposition changes, incrementally revise the master source; one cannot modify only the review report.
