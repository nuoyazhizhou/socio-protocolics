## Core Questions and Dependencies

What is discussed is organisational communication, responsibility acknowledgement, resources in place and recovery; one cannot call the pathogen's biological transmission TCP/UDP. Message sent, received, understood, escalated, decided, executed and fed back are different events; dissemination volume cannot substitute for sustained collaboration.

This group takes P1/P2/P3 as background, with the main clause 016; 003 moves to Group I's application, cross-referenced by specific material. Acknowledgement and feedback may have a cost; reliability can be provided by multiple mechanisms, and one cannot paste TCP or UDP labels by organisational outcome.

## Success/Failure Dimensions, Comparison and Counter-Evidence

Separately measure the time and evidence of first identifiable signal, report, acknowledgement, responsibility handover, materials / personnel arrival, actual execution and feedback. The predetermined task should be a concrete information / resource chain, not "whether the state governs well" as the outcome variable. Sustained-monitoring capacity and a single emergency outcome are listed separately.

Competing explanations include case-discovery opportunity, pathogen and contact structure, resources, professional capability, community cooperation, and political and economic conditions. Do process tracing by the same fields; if a credible in-case comparison exists, design an estimate, and do not identify causation from a cross-national four-cell table.

Evidence that would force a revision: added acknowledgement does not improve execution; resource conditions rather than information feedback explain the difference; formal acknowledgement is rare but another mechanism provides reliable delivery; more complex feedback increases delay. If a model can only call all success "has feedback" and all failure "no feedback", stop that application.
