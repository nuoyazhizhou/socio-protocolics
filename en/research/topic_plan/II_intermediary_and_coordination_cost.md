## Success/Failure Dimensions, Comparison and Counter-Evidence

Record, for every qualified requirement, all relevant person-minutes, calendar delay, first-attempt errors, final qualification, attempt / registration / completion denominator, fee flow, independent review and exit conditions. A reduction in handling count and a drop in total burden are different results; a money transfer does not directly equal the same amount of resource loss.

Candidate explanations include information sharing, duplicate review, specialisation gain, supervision deterrence, agent arbitrage and interest incentive. First make the before/after flow diagram of the same item, then check those who did not enter registration and the anomalous items; if a phased-implementation comparable item can be found, separately assess selection entry and contemporaneous variation, and do not simply call it a natural experiment.

Evidence that would change the verdict: after removal, rework, missed review or exclusion increases; added supervision yields a benefit sufficient to offset the investment; burden increases even without exclusive control; the old process already provided the same function at lower cost. When 004's premise of "no new output / recovery" is not met, that is a rejection of applying that model, not a overturning of its in-assumption product arithmetic.

Recent-neighbour searches pending: transaction cost, organisation coordination, administrative burden, agency and corruption, and the human-factors research on checklists. The engineering representation must be compared with the ordinary process checklist carrying the same information; a word swap itself does not count as a new mechanism.
