# I—VI Group Thematic Implementation Plan

Version: v0.6 | Date: 2026-10-27 | Language: Chinese original; English translation pending.

This directory provides six continuable group plans, the Chinese and foreign candidate positions for each group, and the first task for 19 effective SP-RFC items and two non-numbered materials. The SP-RFC items are consecutively numbered 001—019; 007 is a normative rule, and 019 is the cross-group tool/research norm in Chapter 8. Group II already has conceptual and research material and some real document records, but the real main case has not yet been developed and T3 has not been passed; the task list does not equal a finished draft, an independent experiment, or already-established reasons for success or failure. A/B denote exploratory directions; they do not pre-commit to success or failure, and a single project may simultaneously contain both obstruction and correction.

Group I has a standalone [channel-capacity and semantic-boundary plan](I_channel_capacity_and_semantic_boundary.md) that integrates the existing language-reform candidates, H01—H03, the pending 002 task, and the 003 semantic-propagation application entry; [the case selection and comparison method](../case_selection_and_comparison_method.md) retains the case-selection method and source records, and the [Hu Shi article](../../articles/hu-shih/how_to_evaluate_hu_shis_works.md) retains the currently actual scope of use. Definitions and attribution still follow [Whitepaper §1.5](../../whitepaper/chapter-01.md#-04-协议学核心对象模型与公理体系layer-2-theoretical-primitive-spec).

## Zero. Shared Principles and Stage Status

Before drafting, continuing, retitling, or translating any group, one must re-read that group's newly added "real-case and proposition-testing card" and follow [Case-Driven Writing and Proposition Testing](../../CASE_DRIVEN_TESTING.md). Group I's card is unified in [this group's plan](I_channel_capacity_and_semantic_boundary.md); the case-method page retains only pointers. One must first fix the specific sub-claim, the real main case and its evidence chain, the real counter-material or a sourced competing explanation, and only then form the main text; the aim is to let evidence adjudicate the theory, not to predetermine a positive result.

Existing text and the five local discussion drafts are managed as "conceptual sample chapters / pending reconstruction with a real case"; they are not counted as T3-passed merely because the writing is complete, reads smoothly, or contains a few real citations. The next round prioritises developing the real main case, supplementing contrary material, and submitting proposition receipts, rather than continuing to expand fabricated explanations in bulk. Where groups already have material, that does not mean the real narrative and comparison for this round are complete; existing cases will be re-reviewed later by the same standard.

The reading scope and access gaps of these candidate sources are listed in each group respectively. Official-document confirmation provisions, institutional reporting that supplies a process or the parties' evaluations, and research papers that still require a methods check are three different things; the three cannot automatically substitute for one another as effect evidence. A search-abstract hit, a downloaded file, and a full-text read are different states.

## Two. Unified Execution Steps

1. **Material starting point.** Fix a period, objective, implementer, actual audience and denominator for each case; record the source and version item by item; publicly register known outcomes and the material the author has already read.
2. **First-round coding.** Record using five categories: facts, actor explanations, author mappings, hypotheses, and unknowns; retain contrary material, the source chain and coding disagreements. When one case is used across groups, explain the question separately, but it must not be counted as independent repeated evidence.
3. **Mechanism comparison.** First write the specific process where the mechanism is expected to appear, then compare against ordinary coordination cost, incentives, path dependence, and resource and external shocks; which observation supports which claim must be distinguishable.
4. **Research specification.** Only after obtaining a credible denominator, timeline and comparison conditions should an estimation plan be set separately. A four-cell cross-national table is not a matched experiment; one cannot use a four-case regression to infer a theoretical effect.
5. **Chinese article.** Write the main text only after sufficient sources are in hand, attaching the postulate background, main proposition group / RFC, normative objective, qualitative extension, counter-example and open questions. Do not write the reason for missing evidence as the main cause.
6. **Bilingual synchronisation.** Form a fixed version of the Chinese text and source note first, then produce the English counterpart; retain the same set of cases and claim boundaries. Follow the [bilingual convention](../../BILINGUAL_CONVENTIONS.md) specifically.

## Three. Task Order and Deliverables

The suggested work queue is II → VI → III → IV → V, with Group I's existing material continuing to be supplemented with evidence. The reason is that II has old sample drafts and fabricated material that can be reused, and VI has a definable system switchover and project assessment; III—V require more domain, causal and material verification. This order expresses editorial priority; it does not mean the first two groups are already verified, nor that the other groups must wait for all articles to be finished.

| Stage | What each group must deliver | When to stop advancing to the next stage |
|---|---|---|
| T1 Material table | At least one checkable fact chain completed, with gaps, contrary material and source identity listed | Only a title, abstract or secondary citation, and the alleged event cannot yet be confirmed |
| T2 Comparison draft | Sub-claim, object / window / denominator, real process, real counter-material or a sourced competing explanation, rejection / narrowing conditions | Only hypothetical counter-examples, key variables inferred backwards from the outcome, or main sources citing one another |
| T3 Chinese case draft | A developed real main case, counter-material, engineering mapping, source note, proposition receipt and research entry | Dominated by concepts, simulation or name-dropped real material, lacking a sub-claim verdict, still leaning on unverified facts or a universal explanation; label as a conceptual sample chapter and supplement material |
| T4 English counterpart | The corresponding Chinese version, terminology, and a claim-by-claim check record | The Chinese and English conditions or evidence status differ, or material usability is not stated |

T1 can first be done by one person in roughly one to two weeks as a material trial; it actually depends on archive and data availability; it is not a commitment to complete historical causal research within that time. A successor may submit only material corrections, without needing to agree with the whole framework or commit to a paper.
