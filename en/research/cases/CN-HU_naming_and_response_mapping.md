## Preliminary mapping of the Eight Points and the three-point program

The table below is an editorial analytical correspondence, not a clause-by-clause version mapping ever declared by the two authors, nor a one-to-one equivalence relation.

| Hu Shi's item (H1) | Comparable part in Chen Duxiu (H2) | Overlap and differences that cannot be flattened |
|---|---|---|
| Must have substance | Lyricism, sincerity, realism, and criticism of empty writing | H1's first point explicitly includes emotion and thought, and is by no means merely formal; H2 also organises his own literary categories and social judgements |
| Do not imitate the ancients | Against the stale and archaic, and an orientation toward era/social subject matter | There is overlap; one cannot conclude from this that the two judge all classical works or the realist goal identically |
| Attend to grammar | Clarity, plainness, and criticism of abstruse writing | The grammar requirement relates to audience understandability, but is not an independent grammar clause within H2's three points |
| Do not utter sickly sighs without cause | Lyricism, sincerity, independent self-respect, and criticism of flattery and hypocrisy | Goal-related; H1's fourth point already carries a social and reader orientation of its own; one cannot say the social goal was added entirely by H2 |
| Discard clichés and stock phrases | Against ornamentation, extravagance, flattery, and hypocrisy in applied writing | There is concrete overlap, but each side's literary classification and argumentative basis must still be preserved |
| Do not use literary allusions | Against obscurity and abstruseness and toward a broader audience | Functional comparison only; H2's three-point program is not a verbatim restatement of "do not use allusions" |
| Do not use matched antithetical couples | Criticism of parallel prose, regulated verse, and ornate forms | The specific formal criticism is comparable, but does not directly equal the whole national/social literary goal |
| Do not avoid common characters and colloquialisms | The literary goal of ease, clarity, and plainness | Comparable wording and audience; one cannot equate plainness directly with the vernacular, nor collapse the two texts into a single language norm |

H3's Section 1 reviews and rewrites its earlier items itself, supplying the author's own continuity account of his claims. It is neither a mere change of article title, nor can one infer from the two titles alone that "Hu Shi changed his wording because of Chen Duxiu."

## Two-way check of names and references

| Phenomenon | What this round can confirm | What still cannot be inferred |
|---|---|---|
| Different names, possibly overlapping claims | H1's title uses "reform"; H2's title uses "revolution," and explicitly backs Hu Shi; content shows the overlap and differences above | Not automatically a case of different names fully co-referring; without a complete temporal corpus one cannot say a long coexistence later converged entirely |
| Same author, different article titles and summaries | H3's title differs from H1's, and reviews and reorganises earlier claims | One cannot assert it is merely a relabelling, accepting someone's order, or that an editor unified the wording; motive requires separate checking of correspondence etc. |
| Shared large label, content still differs | One can compare the two authors' programs and goal scope | Content differences do not mean common identification is impossible, nor that coordination necessarily worsens; one must check misunderstanding, mutual reference, and actual action |
| Formal status and actual adoption | H4 instead records that vernacular practice preceded the orthodox status being fought for; school access is checked separately by H03 | Name—recognition—adoption is not a fixed one-way three-stage chain; nor can one infer from the executive order's use of the vernacular script that the agency deliberately suspended the name "national language" |

Important reverse requirement: check not only the reuse of the same name, but also whether different names form a common object through definitions, examples, or direct mutual reference. If the parties clearly understand what each other is talking about, different names can coexist; a valid interface does not require that society be left with only one spelling.

## Separating the necessity claim from the gain claim before rejecting

**N: Uniform word form is a necessary condition for collaboration.** If reliable process material establishes that participants continuously completed the same collaborative task without a uniform word form, this necessity claim can be rejected. It is not a synonymous rewording of SP-RFC-002's existing "can reduce cost" expression.

**G: Among a specified population, with the same definitions and information, and in a specified identification/collaboration task, the label arrangement reduces cost relative to a feasible alternative.** It must be stated whether the comparison is between a uniform label, explicitly-mapped variant names, or same-information expressions without a short label; the name, definition, organisational opportunity, and publication resources cannot be bundled into a single treatment. Indicators separately record joint-reference accuracy, mis-matching, rework, communication time, and task completion; one cannot directly judge that coordination has worsened merely because disagreements of opinion have increased.

Before subsequent effect testing, separately define the applicable population, time window, feasible control, minimum meaningful improvement, and error/power. If the interval of a qualified comparison excludes that improvement, or shows net harm under the premise that task quality does not decline, then reject this local gain prediction. Mere non-significance, failure to find a response, or the fact that some people can collaborate without a uniform word form are all insufficient to reject G. If historical material cannot identify G, report insufficient evidence; do not rename it "already verified to be merely a concomitant phenomenon." At present these parameters are not locked, no effect study has been conducted, and one cannot claim that T2 has been passed.
