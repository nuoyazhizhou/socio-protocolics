# OP-002 Research Notes | Certification Access, Control Rights and Independent Exploration

Version: research design draft v0.1 | Date: 2026-10-27 | Chinese original; English translation pending.

Problem registration, hypothesis decomposition and partial source initial-check are currently complete; there is no complete case sample, parameter measurement, pre-registration, simulation result or causal-effect estimate. This is not a finished case study, nor does it claim that a doctoral dissertation or new paradigm is established. The concise entry is the [Question Card](question_card.md); the reading scope is in [Source and Boundary](source_and_boundary.md).

## 1. Research Question and Position in the Whitepaper

Main question: how do the returns of a certification system, the conditions of authorisation, and feasible alternatives affect an organisation's resource allocation, control rights and exploratory activity? After access is allowed, autonomy may decrease, increase, stay unchanged, or change by dimension.

| Docking position and original identity | What this problem intends to check | What is not thereby proven |
|---|---|---|
| v11.2 §5.1 / [RFC-011](../../../社会系统协议学白皮书.md#sp-rfc-011), authority model and social bridging; [G011](../../../社会系统协议学白皮书.md#rfc-evidence-011) | whether review, remedy, revocation and enforcement arrangements change actual control rights and protection expectations | historical privilege does not equal a modern universal right; one cannot derive investment going to zero from asset risk |
| v11.2 §5.4 / [RFC-014](../../../社会系统协议学白皮书.md#sp-rfc-014), institutional instantiation and normative boundary; [G014](../../../社会系统协议学白皮书.md#rfc-evidence-014) | distinguish external recognition, institutional nesting and direct adoption, and identify the authorisation goal and authority scope item by item | individual exam eligibility does not automatically equal organisational authorisation; the interfaces of church, industry or platform are newly added comparison objects, and do not expand the applicable domain of the state-authorisation clause |
| v11.2 §7.2 / [RFC-018](../../../社会系统协议学白皮书.md#sp-rfc-018), conditional-probability model, empirical screening, and design norm; [G018](../../../社会系统协议学白皮书.md#rfc-evidence-018) | whether upstream qualification and control arrangements affect exploration resources, trial-and-error rights and failure correlation | the number of organisations does not equal the number of independent trials; the same standard does not imply effective-branch collapse; one does not use historical stories to verify the independent-success-probability formula |

P1/O5 provide resource and time constraints, P2/O1 require retaining heterogeneous goals and strategic choices, P3/O3 require distinguishing rules, beliefs and actual actions; O2/O4 help record rules and certification intermediaries. They do not directly imply H1—H4 of this problem, nor do they mean that certification intermediaries necessarily seek rent.

## 2. Separate Object and Measurement First

The unit of analysis first chooses "one organisation's decision within a clearly defined period", and does not mix the individual, family, organisation and entire civilisation as the same subject. An individual obtaining a degree and an organisation being co-opted must each have their own evidence.

| Dimension | Recordable content | Indicator that cannot directly substitute for it |
|---|---|---|
| Certification relation | who recognises what qualification, applicable object, effect scope, alternative recognition and dispute procedure | writing the state, church and professional community all as a single Root CA |
| Returns and protection | which positions, income, funds or relief a qualification affects; who expects these returns and when known | back-calculating "patronage returns overwhelm everything" from observed access behaviour |
| Resource allocation | funding, course hours, personnel time and recruitment flow under a pre-defined calibre; record total and denominator | exam success rate, declarations or institution names alone substituting for actual input |
| Control rights | who respectively holds personnel, curriculum, budget, qualification adjudication, rule modification, revocation, objection and exit | directly encoding "recognised / funded" as "co-opted" |
| Exploratory activity | actually conducted methods and projects, scope of deviation from the standard, failure and stoppage, feedback maturity period | institution count, common textbooks, final winner or prestige directly substituting for innovation capacity |

When retaining "pre-processor" as an engineering shorthand, one must specify which observable preparation or screening activity it compresses; that term cannot serve as the outcome variable itself. K_nominal only denotes the number of organisations in the already-defined sample. This problem does not yet create a computable K_effective; if estimation is needed, first define the task, route differences, control rights and dependence, then state whether it is comparable to the K_active of RFC-018.

## 3. Four Candidate Empirical Hypotheses

The following are candidate directions for a to-be-bounded research domain, not universal laws or locked confirmatory hypotheses. Before seeing the results used for testing, the successor should fix the object, period, comparison conditions, outcome dimensions, error requirements and revision rules; already-seen material is listed separately, and exploratory modifications keep a version.

| ID | Candidate relation and conditions | Material needed for support | Observations that would require rejection or narrowing |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1 Certification and input | in a specified range where feasible access channels exist and other opportunities are comparable, a rise in expected net certification return may increase the organisation's resource share devoted to qualification preparation | return or rule change precedes input; records of decision-makers learning of the change; same-calibre input and opportunity cost; distinguish personnel choice from organisational adjustment | after reaching the pre-specified evidence precision, still excluding the expected direction/magnitude, or input change preceding return change and better explained by market opportunity or funding conditions |
| H2 Authorisation and control | when resource dependence and other conditions are comparable, if the appointment/removal, curriculum-veto or revocation rights attached to recognition are actually exercised, they may reduce the organisation's autonomy on the corresponding matters | clauses, original attribution of rights, specific intervention events and post-enforcement choice changes; cannot rely only on clause text | actual choices were not affected by the corresponding power, or the organisation had no such decision right to begin with; other control arrangements explain the result, then this mechanism is bounded |
| H3 Protective authorisation | if superior recognition grants an enforceable internal-regulatory right and relief against external intervention, it may coexist with autonomy maintenance or increase | actual use and outcomes of rights such as external intervention, appeal/suspension; record each control right separately | rights remain only on paper, cannot be enforced, or are offset by stronger constraints; one cannot afterwards call any failure "protection was still insufficient" |
| H4 Exploration space | only when certification requirements substantively constrain optional tasks, or preparation input crowds out exploration resources with no compensation, is a local prediction of narrowed exploration scope proposed | fund and time flows, actual project and method changes, alternative resources and common failure sources; clearly state whether acting on discovery or screening | heterogeneous exploration is still retained under a common standard, or access expands resources so exploration increases; data of sufficient precision exclude the ex-ante prediction, then reject that local prediction |

These observations are not an already-obtained counter-example list. Real contrary material and its limited support are registered in the source table. Non-significant results, data gaps, unsatisfied scenario and refutations are reported separately. H1 holding does not automatically support H2, and H2 holding does not automatically support H4; access and independence may also coexist.

## 4. First-Round Material and Minimal Task

The main material unit first takes the 1231 University of Paris *Parens scientiarum*: issued by Pope Gregory IX, relevant actors include university teachers and students, the Bishop of Paris and the Chancellor. The initial check translation simultaneously stipulates partial internal regulatory power, suspension under specific conditions, and the licensing procedure and teaching restrictions. The currently checkable result is the rights configuration stipulated by the document; one cannot on this basis claim that autonomy is realised or innovation is produced.

The same-case contrary material is its licensing and teaching-restriction clauses; the Chinese candidate material is Elman's study of the educational-autonomy claim and exam-training tension of Donglin and other private academies. The latter is a positioned second-hand study, and the corresponding academy's account books, coursework and personnel originals have not been obtained. The two cases differ in era, organisational nature and material type, and are first used to calibrate classification, not as a causal contrast or civilisational ranking.

The first deliverable may only check the Paris document: item by item list the presence of right object, grantor, constrained party, additional conditions, remedy and revocation clauses, original-text location and translation divergence. A clause not seen does not equal the corresponding right's absence. Then find one implementation event; if none is found, retain the gap and do not claim T2/T3 completion.

Contemporary universities, professional certification, platforms or open-source organisations may set up separate material units, and must re-confirm actors, rules and results. A similar diagram retains only partial relations, and does not assume all scenarios share the same formal mechanism; do not back-fill parameters from a known outcome.

## 5. Three Independent Research Routes

**Historical or organisational mechanism test.** One may use archive checking, process tracing, inter-organisational comparison or statistical methods suited to the material; first make a material-availability table, then commit to panel or cross-national estimation. Compare existing explanations of elite co-optation, resource dependence, institutional isomorphism and professional value, retaining objects that are un-accessed, mixed and insignificantly changed; do not take only the successful.

**Local formal model or ABM.** Specify agents, information, action space, goals, time and exit constraints; allow choices of access, independent exploration, mixed investment, etc. If net utility has already been defined as access being higher, the access conclusion is only a conditional derivation, not a historical discovery. The simulation checks parameter sensitivity, common shocks and alternative update rules; without independent measurement and external testing, do not treat the generated convergence trajectory as empirically verified.

**Expression and tool gain.** One may refer to [OP-001 Research Notes](../OP-001/research_notes.md) for a separate fair comparison; OP-001 currently has two design arms, and this problem does not turn it into an executed three-arm experiment. If one separately sets A as existing mechanism, B as equivalent-term translation, C as engineering-dependency check, one must balance fact, key questions, training, tools and time, and avoid C monopolising the answer. Use new cases, independent scoring and as-blind-as-possible rating to check omissions, errors, unsupported causation and time spent. Tool gain does not establish historical mechanism, and historical support does not establish that the engineering representation is superior.
