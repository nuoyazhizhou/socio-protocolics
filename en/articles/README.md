All new drafts and continuations of existing drafts must follow [case-driven writing and proposition testing](../CASE_DRIVEN_TESTING.md): a real primary case is developed, real contrary material or evidenced competing explanations are checked, and specific sub-claims are submitted for adjudication; a conceptual sample chapter does not count as passing T3.

The case articles show how questions are raised and checked. They are not a list of proofs of the six core proposition groups. Later cases should include failures, partial successes and cases where the framework does not apply, so as to avoid picking only the stories that happen to work.

The intermediary conceptual sample chapter v0.3 keeps three clearly labelled fictional exercises, and additionally links Chinese institutional material and partial checks of two foreign studies; it does not package the several cases as a matched experiment. The corresponding [OP-001](../research/OP-001/question_card.md) research design still has not been pre-registered, has not recruited, and has obtained no effect data; Chinese first, English translation pending.

The Hu Shi article v0.5 has added a [comparison of goals with current deliverables](hu-shih/how_to_evaluate_hu_shis_works.md#reform-scope), and keeps the [framework endnote](hu-shih/how_to_evaluate_hu_shis_works.md#article-framework), distinguishing primary citations, normative objectives and qualitative extensions. The [3→6→19 index](../framework_and_case_index.md) lists the six thematic lines, the 19 effective items, and the material still to be supplied for each; language reform is only one part of them.

There is also a [Zhihu outreach candidate draft v0.1](hu-shih/zhihu_release_draft.md), which keeps the seven-section skeleton and short paragraphs of the outreach sample essay and unfolds the concrete revision process of _Experiments_ (《尝试集》); §4 adds a thread on the cross-author responses of item 002. The two drafts share a factual bottom line, with the main qualifications kept next to the relevant judgements; the outreach draft's longer sources and adjudications are in its [receipt](hu-shih/source_notes.md#zhihu-receipt). The candidate draft has not yet been released externally; research draft v0.5 has not been wholesale replaced by the desktop draft v0.6; neither draft is marked as having passed T3 acceptance.
