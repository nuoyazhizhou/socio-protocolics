All new drafts and continuations of existing drafts must follow [case-driven writing and proposition testing](../CASE_DRIVEN_TESTING.md): a real primary case is developed, real contrary material or evidenced competing explanations are checked, and specific sub-claims are submitted for adjudication; a conceptual sample chapter does not count as passing T3.

The case articles show how questions are raised and checked. They are not a list of proofs of the six core proposition groups. Later cases should include failures, partial successes and cases where the framework does not apply, so as to avoid picking only the stories that happen to work.

The intermediary conceptual sample chapter v0.3 keeps three clearly labelled fictional exercises, and additionally links Chinese institutional material and partial checks of two foreign studies; it does not package the several cases as a matched experiment. The corresponding [OP-001](../research/OP-001/question_card.md) research design still has not been pre-registered, has not recruited, and has obtained no effect data; Chinese first, English translation pending.
