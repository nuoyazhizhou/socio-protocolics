# The Hu Shi article: sources, mapping and evidence boundaries

Corresponds to [research draft v0.5](how_to_evaluate_hu_shis_works.md) and the [Zhihu outreach candidate draft v0.1](zhihu_release_draft.md); compiled on 2026-10-23. This file is an editor's self-check record and does not pass itself off as independent peer review. Web pages are transcriptions of the original authors' text or institutional publications; obtaining a web page is not the same as completing a collation against the original source. v0.4 completed the framework citations; v0.5 added the §7 comparison of goals and deliverables. The actual reading scope of the newly added sources 11—15 is listed in the table below; the outreach draft keeps the numbering, adds 16, and supplements where source 5b is used. No understanding or coordination gain, and no estimate of the reform's delayed talent effects, has been obtained on this basis.

## Material withdrawn from or set aside by this article

No longer used as evidence in the main text: Ai Wei's scores and sample figures, Qian Xuantong's reading minutes, a uniform encoding-complexity multiple, market shares, a Classical-versus-vernacular efficiency multiple, and years of industrialisation delay — all lacking current primary checking. What is withdrawn here is the under-supported way these were used, not a verdict that these sources or figures are all false.

Statements such as "six hundred years", "the nationwide switch completed in four years", "同数 (same value) means the corresponding value", "grammar contributed the most", and "mutual translatability rules out paradigm change" have been withdrawn or converted into explicit questions. These questions can still be studied, but proportionate evidence must be supplied afresh.

### Observations that would force us to change our judgements

For 001's local-gain prediction, the population, equivalent material, task, minimum improvement and error standard must first be locked down. If a qualified comparison rules out a pre-set gain, or the gain comes only from extra explanation, that local prediction is rejected/narrowed; the current parameters are unset and do not pass themselves off as pre-registration.

002 separates the necessity N of unifying word forms from the gain G of the labelling arrangement. That cooperation remains possible under variant names can reject N, but cannot directly reject G; G must be compared with same-information alternatives, with shared reference, mismatch, rework and completion cost measured separately. An increase in divergence does not automatically equal failure, and a gain not yet identified does not mean "pure by-product" has been proven. See the N/G part of the initial table.

If textbook/classroom material shows the regulation was not implemented, the judgement that regulated entry drives adoption must be narrowed; if works or correspondence show that later authors were not influenced by *Experiments*, success as a demonstration can no longer be recorded on the strength of publication order alone. New material can change the case conclusions; without a proportionate theoretical result, the master source is not changed just to make the write-back fit.

### Adoption boundaries of this outreach rewrite

Adopted: the seven-section skeleton, short paragraphs, the engineering lens, and the closing second-round question and research invitation. Not kept: the 39 bit/s comparison; reading data not yet checked against the original research are not used as filler; "同数" is not guessed to be a known modern concept. The Guo Moruo / Yu Dafu quotations were not located in the originals this time and were therefore not included; this is not a verdict that they are necessarily spurious.

Not adopted: inferences such as "13.1→6.9 represents the average over all Chinese characters", "3500→56008 proves 16-fold damage", "a fixed 1 bit lost", "a nationwide household-registration disaster", or "the two opposing cases have sealed off adaptationist criticism"; nor is Korean Hangul generalised into a failure case. Character-form, morpheme, vocabulary and task-related distinctions, and record traceability, are studied separately.

The two drafts share a factual standard, with key limits kept next to the relevant claims rather than hidden at the end of the text to retract assertions made in the body. Sources and receipts are not exemption passes from argument. Whether the GitHub target address and this content are online must be verified separately; the repository has been pushed and the reading site is live, but publication on external platforms such as Zhihu is not claimed, nor are independent review or a T3 pass claimed.
