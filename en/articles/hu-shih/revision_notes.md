## v0.3 substantive revision record

| Original treatment | Treatment in this version | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| "That ruler itself is wrong" | "This one ruler alone is not enough" | keeps the contribution dimension without cancelling literary evaluation |
| Value / contribution adjudicated separately by critics / history | changed to the materials each requires | the three-way division is an analytical choice, not mutually exclusive disciplinary boundaries |
| Not a single sample of modern poetry before *Experiments* | acknowledges that writing and publication already existed before the collection | avoids treating the collection as the starting point of practice |
| Vernacular "ran for six hundred years", conversion completed in four years | discusses by genre and setting; only says there was institutional push over several years | starting point and implementation target cannot be mixed |
| "Classical Chinese won only on saving space" | adds functions still awaiting comparison, such as written communication across dialects and the continuity of the classics | does not declare an overall win-or-lose on a self-selected single metric |
| Classical and vernacular both 39 bit/s | removed from the comparison table, with the research boundary explained | the speech study does not measure reading comprehension in the two styles |
| A uniform encoding-complexity multiple; AV1 supported only after 2021 | changed to a discussion by implementation and hardware; corrected against the 2020 official material | does not expand specific conditions into a general rule |
| The population as devices with fixed tiers | the analogy stands only for training conditions; people learn and choose for themselves | conforms to the boundary of agent autonomy |
| Technical competition and evolution "independently validating" the vernacular explanation | changed to an analogy that helps raise questions | material from different domains cannot directly validate social causation |
| Classical Chinese losing to the printing press, the telegraph and compulsory education | brings in institutions, schools, markets, political culture and actors | withdraws the single-technology attribution |
| Dictionaries and textbooks "do not count" | restores the original "important but not sufficient on their own" | does not turn an insufficient condition into no effect |
| The norm issued for half a year with nobody moving, follow-up only once the samples appeared | that causal narrative is deleted | conflicts with the earlier draft's own timeline and lacks impact identification |
| Deliberately leaving defects for imitation | distinguishes the historical use of preserving existing works from deliberately manufacturing defects | the fourth-edition preface supports the former, not the latter |
| A four-edition experiment concluding grammar was the main cause | changed to three ways of reading; the terminological question is kept and the ordering withdrawn | information gain, order and background were uncontrolled |
| Classical Chinese unteachable, scientific vernacular lossless | both require training; task effects to be measured separately | style labels are not cognitive effects |
| Whether texts are mutually translatable decides paradigm / protocol revolution | changed into several questions studying migration, compatibility and authority | one dimension does not constitute a sufficient classification criterion |
| Only failure can be predicted; success is logically unpredictable | withdrawn; success and failure alike are subject to condition and outcome testing | prevents the framework from being immunised against part of the contrary evidence |
| Ending in praise of Hu Shi | leaves three open questions and the framework entry | converts attention into concrete contributions that can be taken up |
| The endnote only briefly describing the borrowed terms | v0.3 adds the three modelling starting points, the local mechanism hypothesis and the project's public entry points, noting the corresponding versions have not yet been pushed | helps Zhihu readers find the whitepaper, and distinguishes postulates, hypotheses and case evidence |

## v0.5: comparing goals and deliverables

- A new §7 "Long-term goals and current deliverables: how to keep them apart" is added; the former closing moves to §8; the main article structure is preserved.
- Hu Shi's 1918 arrangement and his 1953 Q&A are used to discuss the concrete implementation scope, without projecting back to early motives and without shaping an image of him as always opposed to simplified characters or phonetic spelling.
- Lu Xun's 1934 trial compositions / mixed writing and Qian Xuantong's 1918 transition arrangements are kept, avoiding a direct verdict that fierce wording implies no implementation layer; Qu Qiubai's concrete scheme still awaits checking.
- Second-round simplification serves only as a cross-period questioning of goals and costs, distinguishing trial use, stopping the trial and formal discontinuation; the full mapping, reading effects and traceability questions remain in the evidence table for further study.
- Both bundling and separation allow contrary outcomes; the 1927 figure or talent-shortage consequences are not pre-filled; unmeasured data do not enter conclusions.
- Citation number 10 in the main text is aligned with the existing HTML source; 11—15 are added with their reading scopes, plus claim receipts for the new paragraphs.
- 002 was not expanded; the whitepaper, CheatSheet and AGENTS were not modified; no whole-piece T3, independent peer review or completed empirical validation is claimed.
