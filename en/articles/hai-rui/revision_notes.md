2. **Dual Historical Slices & Spatio-Temporal Separation (Audit Safeguard 1)**:
   - Strictly separated the **1577 (Wanli 5) Huizhou Shexian Head-Tax Silk Dispute (Shuai Jiamo's bypass appeal and *The Book of Silk*)** from **Hai Rui's fiscal reforms as Magistrate of Chun'an (1558–1562) and Governor of Yingtian (1569–1570)**;

3. **Incorporation of Real Counter-Evidence & Competing Explanations (Audit Safeguard 2)**:
   - Incorporated the **real same-case counter-observation** of Hai Rui's eight-month Yingtian governorship—where vast jurisdictional scale and the absence of a scalable alternative technical bureaucracy led to thousands of daily petitions, administrative overload, and impeachment—rejecting one-dimensional narratives that "personal moral rectitude and iron willpower alone suffice";
   - Incorporated the fiscal history competing explanation that Ming magistrates and clerks received starvation wages and lacked statutory office budgets (meaning part of the *huohao* meltage fee funded essential paper, runners, and silver transport), explicitly constraining `SP-RFC-004` against treating 100% of surcharges as pure parasitic loss.
