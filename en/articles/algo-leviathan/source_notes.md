## 1. Core Archival and Empirical Literature

### 1.1. Robert Owen's New Harmony and Common-Pool Resource Failure
* **Core Fact**: Robert Owen purchased Harmony, Indiana in 1825, establishing an experimental community abolishing private property and currency. Operating smoothly under his personal supervision, it collapsed within 24 months (1827–1828) following his departure due to labor free-riding, ration disputes, and factional fragmentation.
* **Citations**:
  * Owen, Robert (1826). *The New Harmony Gazette*, Vols. 1 & 2. (Original community gazettes, constitutions, and records).
  * Podmore, Frank (1906). *Robert Owen: A Biography*. London: Hutchinson & Co., Chapter 15: "New Harmony".
  * Harrison, J. F. C. (1969). *Quest for the New Moral World: Robert Owen and the Owenites in Britain and America*. New York: Charles Scribner's Sons.

### 1.2. Soviet Gosplan and Goodhart's Law Distortions
* **Core Fact**: Evaluating industrial production via singular physical metrics (total tonnage, piece counts) induced extreme malicious compliance (unusable massive bolts, fragile needles); the 1960s OGAS computerized economic management network stalled due to systematic data fabrication by regional ministries.
* **Citations**:
  * Nove, Alec (1986). *The Soviet Economic System* (3rd ed.). Boston: Allen & Unwin.
  * Gerovitch, Slava (2008). *InterNyet: Why the Soviet Union Did Not Build a Nation-Wide Computer Network*. *Information & Culture*, 43(4), 335–359.
  * Goodhart, C. A. E. (1975). *Problems of Monetary Management: The U.K. Experience*. Reserve Bank of Australia.

### 1.3. Amazon TOT Monitoring and Industrial Labor Strain
* **Core Fact**: Algorithmic second-by-second tracking of Time Off Task (TOT) via handheld scanners automatically issues termination notices; empirical data indicates serious injury rates double the industry baseline and annual worker turnover exceeding 150%.
* **Citations**:
  * Kantor, Jodi, & Weise, Karen (2021-06-15). *The Amazon That Customers Don’t See*. *The New York Times*. (Pulitzer Prize-winning investigative report documenting internal TOT metrics and turnover data).
  * Gutelius, Beth, & Theodore, Nik (2019). *The Future of Warehouse Work: Technological Change in the U.S. Logistics Industry*. UC Berkeley Labor Center.

### 1.4. Diocletian's *Edict on Maximum Prices* and Imperial Contract Collapse
* **Core Fact**: In 301 CE, Emperor Diocletian capped prices across 1,000+ goods and services under penalty of death, resulting in widespread trade strikes, the rise of shadow markets, and the abandonment of farmland.
* **Citations**:
  * Jones, A. H. M. (1964). *The Later Roman Empire, 284–602*. Oxford: Blackwell, Chapter 3.
  * Frank, Tenney (1940). *An Economic Survey of Ancient Rome*, Vol. V: *Rome and Italy of the Empire*. Johns Hopkins Press.
