## Author constructions and mapping

The opening scenarios of the form, testing and backups are fictional illustrations, not the author's employer or first-hand record. The three 100-item exercises and all figures can be recomputed item by item from the [OP-001 material pack](../../research/OP-001/material_pack.md); person-minutes, waiting and fees are not summed together, and the unknown is not recorded as zero. Deleting the box on the chart, the front-desk interface and the back-office services are functional analogies; there is no mathematical proof about institutional efficiency.

"Separating service from authority", "tracing where the work went" and "not leaving out the unregistered" are this package's analytical arrangements. They have research precursors; the original gain must be compared separately. The evaluative targets include rights, accessibility, quality and recovery, and cannot be fixed covertly by a total score with arbitrary weights.
