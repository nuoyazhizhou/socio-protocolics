Version: v0.3 | Date: 2026-10-23 | Chinese original; English translation pending.

This round changed neither the whitepaper, the CheatSheet nor internal texts, produced no effect data, and made no submission or push. The public candidate draft accepts feedback on facts and expression; the historical process and actual results in the same Chinese city still need to be filled in, and the four cases do not constitute a paired success-failure experiment. The next research deliverable is supplementary material and a review checklist, not an announcement that Socio-Protocolics has been proven.

## v0.3: correcting the completion status per the real-case requirements

Per the newly added Article 9 of AGENTS and the case-first rules, this article is marked "conceptual sample chapter, pending reconstruction with a real case", and does not count as passing T3. The checking of the text against existing sources added no new research results; the next step must develop the process around a specific real primary case, check contrary material, and then submit sub-claim–evidence–adjudication–revision receipts. The v0.2 editorial deliverable does not mean this acceptance has been passed; the OP-001 design material keeps its original version and not-yet-experimented identity.
