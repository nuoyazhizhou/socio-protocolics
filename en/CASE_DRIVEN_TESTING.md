# Case-Driven Writing and Proposition Testing

Version: v0.1 | Date: 2026-10-23 | This is an English translation of the Chinese source; the Chinese source remains authoritative.

This convention implements [Article 9 of AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md) and applies to all topics, authors, agents, continuations of earlier drafts and translations. The theoretical definitions remain governed by the [whitepaper](../社会系统协议学白皮书.md).

## The Research Task of an Article

Raise questions through real events, trace mechanisms, then let the evidence decide whether a specific claim holds and where it applies, and assess whether the engineering perspective adds to the judgment. Publicity, attracting like-minded readers and joint discussion may be dissemination goals, but "the framework must be shown to be correct" cannot be a research acceptance condition.

Each article selects one checkable sub-claim and attaches the master-source version, the group / RFC, the specific location, the conditions and the epistemic status of the claim; "Socio-Protocolics makes sense" cannot serve as the proposition to be tested. If a new question emerges the target may be adjusted, but the pre-adjustment version, the known outcome and the reasons must be retained; a retrospective analysis cannot be dressed up as an ex-ante prediction.

| Epistemic status of the claim | What must be checked | What it must not be conflated with |
|---|---|---|
| Formal conditional proposition | Assumptions, quantifiers, derivation and conditions of real-world applicability | Unmet premises do not mean the derivation is wrong; a story fitting the facts does not substitute for proof |
| Empirical mechanism or bridging hypothesis | Independent measurement, process, competing explanations, discriminating observations and error | Correlation does not by itself establish causation; a non-significant result is not by itself a refutation |
| Accounting definition | Units, subjects, periods, omissions, duplication and sensitivity | An identity holding does not mean the real net benefit is positive |
| Normative objective | Values, rights, affected subjects, costs and feasible alternatives | Being effective does not by itself imply it ought to be adopted; a story does not establish a value objective |
| Expressive or methodological gain | A fair comparison with ordinary methods under the same information | New terms, a formula that runs and readers' approval do not amount to a theoretical gain |

## Let the Real Process Carry the Main Line of the Body Text

At least one main case must make clear: who faced what problem in what period, how the original rules operated, what decision or change occurred, how the key process unfolded, what the checkable results were, and which consequences remain unknown. Decisions, actions, quotations and figures must have sources; motives are included only where material exists, as the participants' own statements or as bounded interpretations, and do not fill in inner thoughts, dialogue or unobserved scenes.

Evidence should be traceable to the original author / agency, the document version, the dates of writing and of the event, the chapter / clause / page / table number, the actual reading scope and the source chain. Official documents first establish what the rules prescribe, project records describe a process or a self-assessment, and research papers are used according to their method and denominator; none of them automatically substitutes for actual effects. Where outcome material is missing, mark the gap; successes not yet observed cannot be narrated from a reform notice.

Engineering concepts enter where a relationship needs to be distinguished, stating which properties of the mapping are retained and lost, and comparing whether ordinary explanations have already covered the ground. Do not first lay out a set of abstract concepts and then sprinkle in a few real names. Check at least one piece of real counter-material, an opposing observation within the same case, or a sourced competing explanation, and indicate which judgment it would change; a hypothetical failure counts only as a direction for testing, and not finding a counter-example does not count as support.

Each group continues to look for favourable, unfavourable, partially successful and inapplicable material from China and other countries; no article mechanically pads out four cases. The four cells are not a randomised or matched experiment, and reusing the same case or re-citing the same source does not count as independent replication evidence.

## The Topic Card to Be Re-read When Starting and Continuing a Draft

Each [topic plan](../research/主题计划/README.md) must maintain the fields below; when they change, update the card and its version rather than keeping only the title and a candidate list.

| Field | Required content |
|---|---|
| Target sub-claim | Master-source version, group / RFC, original sentence or specific location, conditions, epistemic status of the claim; applications newly added by the author are marked separately |
| Main case and process | Objects, period, region, actors, original rules, key decisions / process, outcome dimensions, known outcome |
| Evidence chain | The original document and location corresponding to each key fact, the actual reading scope, the current gap |
| Counter-material | Real counterexamples, within-case differences or sourced competing explanations; what has been obtained is kept separate from what is still to be searched |
| Discriminating observations | The observations needed to support, reject or narrow; unmet premises and insufficient measurement are listed separately |
| Comparison with ordinary explanations | What existing explanations already cover and what the engineering representation is intended to add; restatement or zero gain is allowed |
| Theoretical write-back | The specific location to be changed and the reason, and the drafts affected; when the evidence is currently insufficient, write "no theory revision for now" |
| Current stage | Material table, comparison draft, case article or conceptual sample chapter, together with the next specific piece of material |

Separate questions are recorded with separate evidence and verdicts. Citing O4, P3 or a group name does not amount to testing the whole group; checking only the application boundary of 004 cannot at the same time claim that the 005 feedback has been verified.

## Stage Acceptance

- **T1 material table:** at least one traceable chain of real facts, source identity and key gaps. Titles alone, unread links or conjectures do not pass.
- **T2 comparison draft:** the sub-claim and conditions, the within-case process, real counter-material or a sourced competing explanation, and the rejection / narrowing conditions are all present. The mechanism cannot be inferred backwards from a known outcome.
- **T3 Chinese case article:** a real main case carries the narrative, the engineering mapping helps a concrete judgment, and the conclusion is borne by the corresponding evidence; submit the receipt below and a source note. If it is dominated by concepts, simulated accounting or the mere naming of real materials, label it "conceptual sample chapter, pending reconstruction with a real case" — this does not count as passing T3. An exploratory case article may have no causal-effect estimate, but it must delimit its conclusion and not insist on waiting for quantitative conditions that do not exist.
- **T4 English counterpart:** translated after the Chinese passes the corresponding stage; it keeps the same case, conditions, unknowns and verdict, and does not use the translation to strengthen the conclusion.

Where material is insufficient, first carry out a search, an evidence table or a recorded case-selection adjustment that can move the work forward; there is no need to ask repeatedly for approval of writing principles already made clear. If something genuinely cannot be obtained, report the gap; do not fabricate a story. The automated checks can confirm that files, numbering and text exist; they cannot substitute for the substantive reading of narrative, evidence and reasoning.

## The Proposition Receipt Every Article Must Submit

| Sub-claim and position in the master source | Supporting and opposing evidence, with location | Competing explanations and the result of discrimination | Verdict and boundary | Proposed revision location, or reason for leaving it unchanged |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| The one item actually checked in this article; the original text and the author's application are kept separate | The source chain is not counted twice; unknowns are not filled in with zeros | Identical meaning is registered as restatement | Local support / rejection of a specific empirical prediction / narrowing the scope of application / an existing explanation already suffices / insufficient evidence; these may coexist as separate items | Specified down to the SSOT clause and derived drafts, or state that the evidence is currently insufficient for a change |

Adjudicate separately "what the real event shows" and "what the framework adds"; a main case succeeding does not by itself demonstrate that the framework has a gain. Correct factual errors first; only if a theoretical change is genuinely needed, revise the master source, the CheatSheet and the related drafts incrementally and run the [automated checks](../AGENTS.md). If there is no new theoretical conclusion, record that honestly as well; do not revise theory merely to pad out the revision count, and do not edit only the review report.

## Holding Style and Evidence Together

Keep short paragraphs, real puzzlement, sharp judgments, contrasts and engineering metaphors, with key conditions placed next to the judgment they qualify. Fictional leads and illustrative numbers are marked clearly on first appearance and do not occupy the factual main line or carry real-world conclusions. Give sources close to the relevant passage and put longer methods in a source note; if a load-bearing fact has not been verified, change it, switch the case or hold the case article back; "comments welcome" does not replace checking.

A reader should be able both to take away one method of checking and to point out under which observation it fails; dissemination, criticism and joint revision thereby connect to the same research question.


## Registering the Generation Card and the Three Tests Separately

Locate the sub-claim with the [whitepaper generation and evidence cards](../社会系统协议学白皮书.md#rfc-evidence-cards); a case-study receipt must state separately which of necessity, sufficiency and minimality is checked this time and whether the other two have not been checked. A mechanism being absent while the standard is still met can argue against necessity; a mechanism existing while it still fails constitutes a refutation only when its sufficiency conditions are met; minimality requires comparing a reduced or alternative scheme. Real counter-material, hypothetical stress scenarios and unmet premises are registered separately. The desirability of a normative objective, its implementation effect and the formal model each use their corresponding evidence; the positive and negative results of one case cannot jointly be counted as the same prediction being verified.

Cases related to 018 register discovery, screening, deployment and migration separately; a successful branch is not named after the fact according to the eventual winner. Keep all registered branches, unknown outcomes, maturity consequences, screening errors and maintainers' conflicts of interest; migration costs return to 017, and objectives related to state authority return to 014.
