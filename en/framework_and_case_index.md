# From 3 Postulates, 6 Core Proposition Groups and 19 Effective SP-RFC Items: An Entry Point for Articles and Research

Version: index v0.8 | Date: 2026-10-26 | Language: Chinese original; English translation pending.

This is a writing and research navigation aid; it does not establish a separate set of definitions. Names, ownership and conditions follow [Whitepaper §1.5](whitepaper/chapter-01.md#-04-协议学核心对象模型与公理体系layer-2-theoretical-primitive-spec); the RFC links lead into the main text. 3 → 6 → 19 expresses modelling dependency and clause ownership; it does not mean that all propositions have been strictly established from the three postulates.

## I. How the three tiers connect to the articles

- The three postulates provide the starting point for modelling. P1 checks the resource and cost boundary; P2 preserves the agent's autonomous objectives; P3 signals the connection among rules, beliefs, incentives and actions. Articles list only the dependencies they actually use; one cannot write a citation of a postulate as though it were verification of that postulate.
- The six core proposition groups organise different questions. Each article chooses a main topic and a few cross-cutting topics, and does not cram every case into language reform or protocol modification.
- The 19 effective SP-RFC items provide concrete analytical entry points: 007 is a cross-group normative rule, 003 is a semantic application, and 019 is the Chapter 8 tool/research norm; former 006/010 were converted to non-numbered material while retaining their research tasks. One must write out the specific parts actually used, and cannot claim a mechanism merely by keyword.

Special note: using P3 to discuss recognised expectations does not equal having used the third group's intertemporal performance model; discussing a trial does not equal having tested 018's independent branch-probability model.

There is no mechanical requirement to assemble four articles for every SP-RFC, nor may one infer that each clause has independent evidence merely because a single article touches several clauses.

## III. The precise coverage scope of the Hu Shi case

The objects use O1/O2/O3/O4; the postulates P1/P2 serve as background, and P3 organises the to-be-tested recognition–expectation–action mechanism. The main topics are 001 of Group I and 014 of Group IV; 007 states the normative objective, and 017/018 are only qualitative extensions.

The intermediary mechanism of 004, 005 (including the merged institutionalised scenario), the candidate process diagram (non-numbered material) and 006 has not been tested by this article; Group III and Group V are not developed; 011, 012, 013, 015, 019 and the like also cannot be counted as covered. The complete [item-by-item citation note](articles/hu-shih/source_notes.md#correspondence-with-the-whitepaper) and the [article endnotes](articles/hu-shih/how_to_evaluate_hu_shis_works.md#article-framework) state the actual use and its limits.

Therefore, "has an article citation" in this index only means that one traceable usage exists; it does not mean that the core propositions, the group they belong to, or the related SP-RFC has gained empirical verification.

## V. Citation conventions for each article

The body's endnotes retain a readable summary: modelling background, main groups and clauses, normative objective, qualitative extension, untested parts, and research entry points. The source note then lists item by item "number — main-text position — role — boundary". External literature and framework citations are kept separate: historical sources support historical facts, and the whitepaper supplies the author's analytical language; mutual links do not form self-evidence.

Local empirical support is discussed only when conditions, measurement, comparison and actual results are attached. Definitions, formal derivations, empirical mechanisms and normative choices each bear their respective obligations; one cannot uniformly list all SP-RFC items as theorems already verified by the narrative.

This page uses the v11.2 numbering. The independent main-text section of each clause is consistent with the [version concordance](./V11_2_NUMBERING.md); the same number in an older version does not automatically denote the same clause.
