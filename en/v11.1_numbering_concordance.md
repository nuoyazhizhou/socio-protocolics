# v11.1 Numbering and Section Concordance

2026-10-26 | Historical record: this page preserves the numbering and sections of v11.0 → v11.1. The current version uses the [v11.2 concordance](./V11_2_NUMBERING.md); the links below are limited to the v11.1 entry, and the section numbers in the tables remain at their then-current positions. v11.1 performed only structural reorganisation, numbering synchronisation and clarification of the epistemic status of claims; it does not represent new empirical results.

## Numbering rules

In v11.1, SP-RFC-001—021 were arranged continuously across the six groups; each had its own independent main-text clause section. v11.0's 014 had already been merged into its 004, and v11.1 registered it uniformly as 005. Old and new numbers are not mixed: for example, v11.0's 019 was a textual-interpretation application, whereas v11.1's 019 is migration accounting.

The `sp-rfc-XXX` anchors in v11.1 carried the meaning of this page; the same-named anchors in the current main text now carry the v11.2 meaning. To track the clauses on this page use `legacy-v11-1-rfc-XXX`; for earlier versions use `legacy-v11-0-rfc-XXX`. We cannot guarantee that version-less same-number links from before still point to the same content; citations should list the version and the clause name.
