### How Each Round Closes

1. **Fix the scope.** Record the block ID, subsection or RFC, the original sentence and this round's question. First read with the [chunking tool](CHUNK_READING_GUIDE.md), then supplement the definitions, generation cards, code or sources relevant to the judgement; there is no need to re-read all entry files every round.
2. **Discuss the options.** State clearly what the original text is meant to solve, where it is unclear or out of bounds, and the optional changes and their costs. Engineering analogies, normative choices, conditional derivations and empirical hypotheses are discussed separately; differences of style are not misjudged as theoretical errors.
3. **Record conclusions and write-ins separately.** Note briefly what is kept, rewritten, split, moved to candidate research, awaiting material or not adopted, and why. Pure discussion does not automatically change the body text; once the user explicitly asks for implementation, the authorised changes are completed without repeated confirmation.
4. **Complete the necessary synchronisation.** Check the affected clauses, generation cards, CheatSheet, source tables, shared internal norms and the different-purpose drafts; if nothing is affected, record the reason for not changing. Tags, anchors and content outside the blocks are preserved; whole-block replacement is previewed first and the hash checked.
5. **Recheck and continue.** Record the actual change locations, the designated check results, unresolved questions and the next subsection; update the corresponding row of this table. Full conversations and line-by-line working drafts stay local, and the public table keeps only concise adjudications; 「本轮已复核」 ("reviewed this round") does not mean the mathematics, historiography or social empirics are all complete.

Each round may use one short receipt: `范围｜结论及理由｜正文是否已改｜受影响材料｜检查及未检查项｜未决问题｜下一项`. Where there are multiple disputed points, adjudicate them separately; a long report is not required every round.
