## Ledger Maintenance Fields

Each citation addition records: the claim's version and body-text anchor, the original and its version/date, the chapter page numbers or table numbers, the actual reading range, variables and denominators, identification assumptions, the original author's claim, this book's interpretation, supporting and contrary material, gaps, and the observations that would change the verdict next time. Statuses are used separately: 「原文定位已核」 ("original located and checked"), 「估计未复现」 ("estimate not reproduced"), 「材料缺口」 ("material gap"), 「效果尚未完成验证」 ("effect not yet through verification"); when a source or version is replaced, keep the differences, and the evidence grade is not upgraded merely because a link exists.
